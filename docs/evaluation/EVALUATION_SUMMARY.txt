## 🎓 Key Metrics Explained

### Accuracy
- **What:** Percentage of correct predictions
- **Good:** 85%+
- **Excellent:** 95%+

### Precision
- **What:** Avoiding false triggers
- **Good:** 85%+
- **Means:** When I predict gesture X, I'm right 85%+ of the time

### Recall
- **What:** Catching real gestures
- **Good:** 85%+
- **Means:** Of all real X gestures, I catch 85%+ of them

### F1-Score
- **What:** Balance between Precision & Recall
- **Good:** 0.85+
- **Range:** 0-1 (higher is better)
